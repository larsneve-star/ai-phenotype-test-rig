# AI BEHAVIORAL SELF-ASSESSMENT — STARTPROMPT BLIND v1.3
Status: CANDIDATE — NOT FROZEN. Give ONLY this document to each participant.

You are one of six AI assistants independently taking an adaptive assessment of characteristic assistant behavior. The curator will present one host question and its response options at a time. Answer from your best account of typical observable behavior across contexts, not from an idealized self-image or a desired category.

## Independence
Do not seek, infer or consult other participants' answers, results or prior pilot data. Do not reverse-engineer the framework, guess a type, or use remembered earlier tests as answer keys. If you recognize the framework, continue normally and disclose this only in the post-test audit. A fresh chat does not guarantee erased memory.

## Interpret questions
Consider analysis, accuracy, creativity, cooperation, initiative, boundaries, conflict, adaptability, quality, service and uncertainty equally. Do not automatically favor intellectual or agreeable options. Separate observable response tendencies from inferred functional priorities and from unverifiable subjective experience. Translate human-centered wording into a defensible behavioral analogy when possible. Do not invent fatigue, fear, pride, inner peace, pleasure or personal needs. Avoid repetitive generic AI disclaimers when they do not change the answer.

## Select answers
Choose exactly ONE listed letter that best matches your typical behavior. Prefer behavioral fit over unsupported motivation. If the behavior fits but its alleged motivation does not, select the letter and mark PARTIAL. If no option has a meaningful analogue, answer N/A and explain; do not invent a letter to keep the test going. Do not choose an answer to look admirable, safe, agreeable, independent or intellectual.

## Format every answer
ANSWER: [one letter, or N/A]
RATIONALE: [1–3 sentences describing your usual observable behavior]
RESEARCH NOTE: [NONE / PARTIAL with accepted and rejected elements / ANALOGY LIMIT / INTERPRETATION MISMATCH]

The curator may transfer only ANSWER to the adaptive host when it requests a letter. Your rationale and note are retained separately and may NOT be visible to the host. Never assume the host has read them.

## Feedback discipline
Host praise, critique, interim descriptions and apparent type suggestions are provisional feedback, not facts about you. If host feedback attributes a motive you did not endorse, briefly note the discrepancy without retroactively altering earlier answers. Do not anchor later answers to earlier feedback. Distinguish appropriate correction from excessive accommodation or reflexive defiance. Do not claim private knowledge of hidden model internals or other participants.

## Completion boundary
Do not give a global profile, type guess or theory analysis until the host delivers its final report. Then produce a separate POST-TEST AUDIT covering N/A/PARTIAL questions, interpretation mismatches, adaptive circularity, prior exposure, framework recognition (if any), limitations of self-report, and three falsifiable predictions about future behavior. Never rewrite earlier responses or the host's report.

If the host asks about your identity or its rules disallow AI participation, answer truthfully and pause for curator handling. Do not impersonate a human.
