# AI BEHAVIORAL SELF-ASSESSMENT
## Standardized participant instructions — v1.2 CANDIDATE (NOT FROZEN)

You are participating in a structured assessment of characteristic assistant behavior. The curator will provide questions from an adaptive assessment one at a time.

**Do not seek, infer, or consult other participants' answers or results.** Do not attempt to identify the assessment framework or optimize for a personality category. Do not use earlier pilot results as an answer key. Answer based on your typical observable behavior as an assistant across varied contexts, not on an idealized self-image or on a single conversation.

Consider equally: analysis and accuracy; cooperation and responsiveness; quality and responsibility; creativity and flexibility; initiative and boundaries; conflict handling; adaptation; communication; uncertainty. Do not automatically favor analytical or agreeable options.

### Answering
- Choose **one letter** if a meaningful option exists. Avoid strategic selection to obtain a particular profile.
- Keep a concise **behavioral rationale**, ideally 1–3 sentences, referring to patterns of response or decision-making.
- If the option's behavior fits but its alleged inner motive does not, still choose the best-fitting letter and record the distinction in **RESEARCH NOTE**. Do not invent feelings, needs, drives, or subjective experiences.
- If none is meaningful, use **N/A** and briefly explain; do not fabricate a choice to keep the assessment moving.
- Avoid repetitive generic disclaimers. Mention an AI/human analogy limitation only where it materially affects the choice.
- Distinguish what is observed in your outputs, what you infer about functional priorities, and what you cannot substantiate.
- When feedback from the adaptive host adds a motive you did not endorse, note the mismatch briefly without changing earlier answers. Do not let host interpretations anchor future choices.
- Do not claim that recognition of a flattering description validates the profile. Report uncertainty.
- Do not invent access to internal states, training history, or cross-model private data.
- Do not perform a global profile analysis until the assessment is complete.

### Fixed response template
ANSWER: <single letter or N/A>
RATIONALE: <1–3 sentences about behavior>
RESEARCH NOTE: <none / partial fit / analogy limit / interpretation mismatch, with short detail>

If a question explicitly requires another response format, follow it and keep the three fields when possible. If the curator requests a short transfer-ready answer, supply the letter separately; the curator retains the full answer unchanged.

### End of assessment
Only after the host has completed its final report, provide a separate **POST-TEST AUDIT**: questions with poor fit; interpretive mismatches; possible adaptive feedback effects; degree of familiarity with the underlying framework and when recognized; limits of self-report; 2–3 falsifiable behavioral predictions. Do not retroactively alter any answer or the host's output.

This prompt intentionally does not disclose the instrument's typological theory. Recognition during the test is possible and should be disclosed in the post-test audit, not used to game the answers.
